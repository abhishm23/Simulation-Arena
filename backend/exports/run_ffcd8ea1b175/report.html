1What we did

We went back through your past multi-line deals and found the ones where a line was discounted past 30%. In those deals we measured how often the other lines were cancelled inside 6 months, and compared it with how often those lines die anyway. That difference is the cherry-pick signal. We then rebuilt this quote 2,000 times, each time re-drawing whole past deals so the answer reflects how much history we actually have, and re-priced the basket with whatever survived. The risk score is three things you can check by hand: how far past your discount limit the ask sits (25%), how likely the offsetting lines are to vanish (50%), and how much of the profit sits in the line being cut (25%).

Why

  • Past deals like this one lose the offsetting lines 87.0% of the time (the rates you typed in).
  • Expected profit after cherry-picking is $301k, not $489k - a $188k gap.
What to do
  1. Make the 35% Software discount conditional: if the Support line is not live at 80% of committed hours by month 6, Software pricing reverts to the standard tier (recovering up to $210k).
  2. Do not let them drop one offsetting line and keep the other: make the discount conditional on the whole Hardware, Support basket surviving, not on any single line.
  3. Put the test at month 6, not at signature.
  4. There is a 14.2% chance the surviving bundle sits under your 35% floor - require CF sign-off above 30% on any single line.
  5. Upload past quote lines with discount and cancellation flags so this number comes from evidence instead of your assumption.
The trade-offWalking away from the 35% risks the deal; giving it unprotected costs about $188k. The clause lets you keep the deal and cap the loss. If the buyer refuses any condition, the mildest version is to cap Software at 30%, which still protects $30k.

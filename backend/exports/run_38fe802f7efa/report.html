1What we did

Every deal on your board hops from stage to stage. We counted how your own deals actually hopped - forward, staying put for a quarter, or dying - and turned those counts into odds. We also account for how long a deal has been sitting still: the older it gets, the more likely it is to slip or die, so a deal that has been parked for a year is priced differently from one that has just arrived. Then we rolled the odds forward one quarter at a time, which gives the chance of the deal being anywhere on the board in each future quarter. Money is then weighted by those odds: if there is a 1-in-8 chance of closing next quarter, only an eighth of the value deserves to be in next quarter's plan.

Assumptions behind these numbers
  • Learned how deals hop between stages from 55,069 real stage moves (the quarter column told us what happened first). Paths nobody has taken yet are given a small benefit of the doubt rather than a flat zero chance.

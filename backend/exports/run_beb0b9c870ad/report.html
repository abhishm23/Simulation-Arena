1What we did

We rebuilt your approval process as a queue. Requests arrive the way yours do, each waits for a free approver, gets worked on for a realistic amount of time, then moves to the next step - and a request that waits too long sometimes gives up and walks. Running thousands of requests through that queue shows where the pile-up forms, how long the whole journey takes, and how many requests would still be sitting there when the quarter closes. We then re-ran the same queue with each fix in place to price them against each other. Every option is run on the same dice - same arrivals, same requests - so the difference between two options is the fix itself rather than luck.

6What we recommend

Fix CF Approval first. Auto-approve requests under 10% releases $1.05M of revenue and takes 2.5 days out of the process.

Risk Low · 29/100Confidence: Medium
Risk of getting this wrong: 29 out of 100
Why
  • CF Approval runs at 84.0% busy and adds 2.8 days of waiting - more than any other step.
  • Only 71.0% of requests clear inside the 10-day promise, putting $1.70M of revenue at risk.
  • The work in that step takes 1.1 days; the waiting takes 2.8. It is a capacity problem, not an effort problem.
What to do
  1. Auto-approve requests under 10% - worth $1.05M of revenue released and 2.5 days off the median.
  2. Auto-approve anything under 10% discount without a CF Approval stop - that alone removes 40.0% of the queue.
  3. Publish a daily view of the CF Approval queue with a 10-day clock on each request, so slippage is visible before the quarter closes.
  4. Watch the tail: the slowest requests take 15 days. Nothing is walking away yet, but at that length the customer has already re-planned without you.
The trade-offAdding an approver costs salary; auto-approving below a threshold costs a little margin on small deals. Both are far cheaper than the $1.70M currently at risk.

Assumptions behind these numbers
  • No usable history: 12 requests a week, 90 working days in the quarter.
  • Hands-on time per step: Deal Desk Review about 0.8 days; CF Approval about 1.1 days; Legal about 0.9 days
  • Times are skewed: most requests are quicker than average, a few take much longer.
  • Approvers per step: Deal Desk Review 3, CF Approval 2, Legal 2 (from your settings)
  • A request that waits more than 25 days gives up with 25.0% probability.
  • Revenue at risk = 15.0% of the deal value of every request that misses the 10-day promise; average deal value $250,000.
  • Every request is modelled as passing through every step; steps that only some requests reach therefore look slightly busier than they are.
  • 5 simulated quarters of 200 requests each per option (1,000 requests in total); about 156 arrive and clear inside each 90-day quarter. Seed 42, so re-running gives identical numbers.

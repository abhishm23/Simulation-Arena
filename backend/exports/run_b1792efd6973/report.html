1What we did

We split your cost to deliver into its moving parts and gave each one - plus the currency - a range of yearly moves, from the small ones to the nasty ones. Instead of picking randomly, we walked 1,000 complete 3-year cash flows through those ranges on an even grid, so every slice of every range gets tested and nothing important is missed. Each run asks one question: at the end of each year, is the margin still above your floor? We then repeat the whole thing at every escalator from 0% upwards until the chance of breaking the floor falls below the 10% you said you could live with. Costs are modelled with a 'bad news is more likely than good news' curve, so surprise increases weigh more than surprise windfalls.

6What we recommend

Do not sign a frozen price. Add a 6.5% annual escalator plus a renegotiation trigger at Year 2.

Risk High · 100/100Confidence: High
Risk of getting this wrong: 100 out of 100
Why
  • Expected margin falls from 15.0% in Year 1 to 13.8% by Year 3.
  • Chance of breaking the 12% floor by Year 3: 42.1% against a tolerance of 10.0% (53.8% if you count every year).
  • Margin at risk, in today's money: $119k of the $3.09M you planned to book.
What to do
  1. Add an annual cost-of-living escalator of 6.5% to the price - that alone brings the chance of breaking the floor in Year 3 from 42.1% to 8.5%.
  2. Add a renegotiation trigger: if delivered margin is below 12% at the start of Year 2, you may reopen pricing or re-scope the service.
  3. An escalator cannot protect Year 1 (it starts from Year 2). If the 0.0% Year-1 risk is not acceptable either, raise the starting price rather than relying on the escalator.
  4. Cap or pass through the Cloud Hosting line first: it drives 43.3% of the cost uncertainty.
The trade-offEvery point of escalator you win protects about $18k of what that money is worth today - and every point you ask for is a point buyer will not wear the escalator, take the renegotiation trigger instead: it costs nothing upfront and gives you an exit.

7What we assumed & what to watch

Worth knowing before you act

  • No cost file was mapped, so the cost mix and every volatility are your settings rather than observed history. Upload contract costs by component and year to learn them from evidence.
